# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- Debug 6502 programs on MacBeeb's BBC Micro: breakpoints in your source (with conditions, hit counts and log points), stepping over, into, out of and back, reverse continue, the call stack, registers and flags to edit, memory, disassembly, and data breakpoints (watchpoints).
- BeebAsm and Baron sources: press F5 and MacBeeb assembles them, puts the disc in drive 0 and runs it, with breakpoints on your source lines.
- ca65/ld65 programs, with ld65's debug info for source lines.
- BBC BASIC programs (.bas): breakpoints on lines with conditions in BASIC, stepping a statement at a time, PROC and FN calls, variables and arrays, and stopping on errors.
- The Model B, B+, Master 128 and Master Compact, and the 6502 Second Processor.
- Syntax highlighting for ca65, BeebAsm and BBC BASIC.
